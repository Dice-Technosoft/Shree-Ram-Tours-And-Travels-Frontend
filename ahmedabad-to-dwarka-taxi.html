Key Places Covered in Dwarka Tour

Your private taxi driver will cover all major temples and attractions during your visit.

1. Dwarkadhish Temple

Main temple dedicated to Lord Krishna in the heart of Dwarka city.

2. Bet Dwarka

Sacred island reached via Okha ferry or Sudama Setu bridge.

3. Nageshwar Jyotirlinga

One of the 12 holy Lord Shiva Jyotirlingas located near Dwarka.

4. Rukmini Devi Temple

Historic 12th-century temple dedicated to Lord Krishna's chief queen.

5. Gomti Ghat & Sangam

Holy bathing ghat where Gomti river meets the Arabian Sea.

6. Shivrajpur Beach

Blue Flag certified pristine beach located just 12 km from Dwarka.
